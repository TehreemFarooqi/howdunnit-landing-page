04 NO HAND-WAVING

Local-first.
Not a black box.

Here’s what stays on your PC—and what leaves it when you use the app.

STAYS HERE 01

Your project index

Your code and Claude Code history are indexed and kept on your PC. The index is built locally.

WHEN YOU ASK 02

Your question, with context

Your question and a few redacted code or history excerpts go to the AI provider you choose. During a meeting, recent heard turns go too.

WHEN YOU LISTEN 03

Audio, only while on

After consent, system audio goes to Deepgram for speech-to-text while a meeting is active. The transcript stays in memory and is dropped when it ends.

No account. No telemetry. API keys encrypted by Windows.With local Ollama as your only provider, questions stay on your PC. Read the full privacy details ↗

Meeting audio is a recording. Tell people you’re transcribing and follow your local consent laws.
