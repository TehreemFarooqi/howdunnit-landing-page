Your project stays yours.

The project index stays on your PC. To answer a question, selected redacted source excerpts can go to the AI provider you choose.

Bring your own API keys. Howdunnit is free and open source; AI and transcription provider usage may cost extra.

While listening, meeting audio goes to your configured transcription provider. Screen-capture Privacy Mode is controlled in the app.
